# TC-03 — Family play strikes

**Area:** Gameplay  
**Type:** Manual

## Setup

A board is showing (question visible). Family is playing (after showdown).

## Steps

1. Click **Family play X (1 of 3)**. Audience shows a single X overlay; host hint reads strikes 1 / 3. Button label becomes **Family play X (2 of 3)**.
2. Click again → 2 of 3, then 3 of 3. At 3, steal is open; Family play X disables.
3. **Undo** once. Strike count drops; steal is no longer open.

## Expected

Only Family play X increments the 3-strike count. Overlay text uses repeated X characters matching the family-play count.
